SeedMask is intentionally narrow: **Bitcoin** and **Kaspa**. That is not a temporary marketing line — it is a product boundary.

Every chain you add expands confirmation UX, address formats, fee markets, and review surface. A hardware wallet that tries to be everything tends to confirm weakly and ship slowly.

By staying on two chains we can keep signing screens clear, keep the Coordinator watch-only model consistent, and spend engineering time on the air-gapped path that actually protects keys.

When hardware and Coordinator downloads open, that same focus will show up in the interface: fewer distractions, sharper reviews, Bitcoin and Kaspa done properly.
